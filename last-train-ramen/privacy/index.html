Privacy Policy

Last Train Ramen · Midnight Snack Games · Last updated: September 25, 2026

Short version: Last Train Ramen is a single-player cooking game. You don’t create an account, and we never ask for your name, email or contacts. Your progress is stored on your device (and, if you sign in, in your own Google Play Games or iCloud account). The Game sends anonymous gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics) and crash reports (Sentry), and sends in-app purchase receipts to our server to verify them. It does not show ads and does not collect your advertising identifier. Each service is described below. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

Last Train Ramen (“the Game”) is developed and published by Midnight Snack Games (“we”, “us”), the data controller for the purposes of this policy. Contact: hello@midnightsnack.games. Our postal contact details are available on request by email.

What the current version does

  • Game progress and settings (your nights, coins, decorations, language, sound settings) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Your phone’s own system backup (for example, Android Backup to your Google account) may include them; that backup is run by your device provider, not by us.
  • Photos you take in the Game leave your device only if you share them yourself through your phone’s share menu, to the app you choose.
  • Game settings file. The Game occasionally downloads a small settings file from our website (midnightsnack.games) so we can switch features off or ask you to update if a version has a serious bug. The request contains no information about you or your game. As with any web request, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address and standard request details (such as the version of the web engine the Game runs in) to deliver the file, and may keep them in its server logs; we do not receive or store them.
  • Google Play features (Android). The Game may ask Google Play to show a rating prompt or to offer an update. These features are provided by Google Play under Google’s Privacy Policy; we do not receive any data from them.

We do not collect real names, email addresses, precise location, photos, contacts or advertising identifiers.

Services the Game uses

Currently active: crash reports (Sentry), gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics), cloud save (Google Play Games / iCloud) and in-app purchases. Video ads (Google AdMob) are off.

If a future update turns on a service that is off, or changes what an active one sends, we will update this page and the store listing’s data disclosures before that update is released, and the “Last updated” date above will change.

Crash reports (Sentry)

When the Game crashes we receive the error message, the technical stack trace, the names of the last in-game actions (for example, “night started”, without any details), the Game’s version and the time. Crash reports contain no account, name or advertising identifier. Sentry receives your IP address in order to deliver the report; we configure it not to store it. Crash reports are kept for up to 30 days. See Sentry’s Privacy Policy.

Gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics)

Anonymous gameplay events (a night started or ended, an item bought with in-game coins, a tutorial finished), a random installation identifier created by the Game (not your advertising ID), your device model, operating system version, language, the Game’s version and, if the Game was installed from a store link with a campaign tag, the name of that campaign. GameAnalytics receives your IP address in order to deliver the events. We use it to understand which parts of the Game players enjoy or find confusing. See GameAnalytics’ Privacy Policy.

Optional video ads (Google AdMob) — off

If enabled: the Game shows a video ad only when you choose to watch one for an in-game bonus. Google’s ad service then processes your device’s advertising identifier, IP address (approximate location) and ad interactions to show and measure the ad. In the UK and EEA we ask for your consent first through Google’s consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in the Game’s Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Cloud save (Google Play Games / iCloud)

Only if you sign in (Google Play Games on Android, iCloud on iPhone): a copy of your game progress is stored in your own Google Play Games or iCloud account so you can continue on another device. We do not receive your account details or the copy itself. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive a purchase confirmation needed to deliver what you bought; we never receive your payment details. To stop fake purchases and to take back currency from refunded ones, the game sends the store's purchase token (or transaction id) and the product id to our purchase-check server (Cloudflare Workers, ltr-purchases.imposter-site-counter.workers.dev), which asks Google Play or Apple about it. The server keeps no account and no device id; it stores only a one-way hash of tokens that were refunded, for about 14 months.

How we use information

  • To provide the Game and keep it stable.
  • To fix crashes and to understand how the Game is played and improve it (Sentry, GameAnalytics).
  • To deliver what you buy and to stop fake or refunded purchases (our purchase-check server).

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interests in keeping the Game working (the settings file), in crash diagnostics and anonymous gameplay analytics, and in preventing purchase fraud; on the performance of our contract with you to deliver what you buy; and, if video ads are ever turned on, on your consent for personalised advertising, collected through Google’s consent form, which you may withdraw at any time.

Service providers & international transfers

We share limited data only with providers that process it on our behalf and only for the services that are active: GitHub (website hosting), Google Play and the Apple App Store, Sentry (crash reports), GameAnalytics (gameplay analytics), Cloudflare (our purchase-check server) and — only if video ads are ever turned on — Google AdMob. Each processes data under its own privacy policy. For EEA/UK users, transfers outside the EEA/UK rely on adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your choices & rights

  • Uninstalling the Game deletes all progress stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to certain processing and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • To exercise a right, email hello@midnightsnack.games. Because the Game has no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find data linked to your installation.

Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

Data retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete the Game or clear its data. Crash reports are kept for up to 30 days; analytics are kept only as long as needed to improve the Game, then deleted or aggregated; refunded-purchase hashes are kept for about 14 months. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your installation, email hello@midnightsnack.games.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date on this page.

Contact
