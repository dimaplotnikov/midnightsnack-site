Privacy Policy

Last Train Ramen · Midnight Snack Games · Last updated: September 23, 2026

Short version: Last Train Ramen is a single-player cooking game. You don’t create an account, and we never ask for your name, email or contacts. Your progress is stored on your device. The current version of the Game does not collect personal data, does not show ads and does not contain analytics. Some optional services may be turned on in future updates; they are described below, and this page will say which of them are active before any update that turns one on is released. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

Last Train Ramen (“the Game”) is developed and published by Midnight Snack Games (“we”, “us”), the data controller for the purposes of this policy. Contact: hello@midnightsnack.games. Our postal contact details are available on request by email.

What the current version does

  • Game progress and settings (your nights, coins, decorations, language, sound settings) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Your phone’s own system backup (for example, Android Backup to your Google account) may include them; that backup is run by your device provider, not by us.
  • Photos you take in the Game leave your device only if you share them yourself through your phone’s share menu, to the app you choose.
  • Game settings file. The Game occasionally downloads a small settings file from our website (midnightsnack.games) so we can switch features off or ask you to update if a version has a serious bug. The request contains no information about you or your game. As with any web request, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address to deliver the file and may keep it in its server logs; we do not receive or store it.
  • Google Play features (Android). The Game may ask Google Play to show a rating prompt or to offer an update. These features are provided by Google Play under Google’s Privacy Policy; we do not receive any data from them.

We do not collect real names, email addresses, precise location, photos, contacts or advertising identifiers.

Optional services in future updates

Currently active: none of the services in this section.

The services below are prepared in the Game but switched off. If a future update turns one on, we will update this page and the store listing’s data disclosures before that update is released, and the “Last updated” date above will change.

Crash reports (Sentry) — off

If enabled: when the Game crashes we receive the error message, the technical stack trace, the names of the last in-game actions (for example, “night started”, without any details), the Game’s version and the time. Crash reports contain no account, name or advertising identifier. Sentry receives your IP address in order to deliver the report; we configure it not to store it. Crash reports are kept for up to 30 days. See Sentry’s Privacy Policy.

Gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics) — off

If enabled: anonymous gameplay events (a night started or ended, an item bought with in-game coins, a tutorial finished), a random installation identifier created by the Game (not your advertising ID), your device model, operating system version, language and the Game’s version. We use it to understand which parts of the Game players enjoy or find confusing. See GameAnalytics’ Privacy Policy.

Optional video ads (Google AdMob) — off

If enabled: the Game shows a video ad only when you choose to watch one for an in-game bonus. Google’s ad service then processes your device’s advertising identifier, IP address (approximate location) and ad interactions to show and measure the ad. In the UK and EEA we ask for your consent first through Google’s consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in the Game’s Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Cloud save (Google Play Games) — off

If enabled and you sign in to Google Play Games: a copy of your game progress is stored in your own Google Play Games account so you can continue on another device. We do not receive your Google account details. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases — off

If enabled: purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive a purchase confirmation needed to deliver what you bought; we never receive your payment details.

How we use information

  • To provide the Game and keep it stable.
  • If the services above are enabled: to fix crashes, to understand how the Game is played and improve it, and to show optional ads you choose to watch.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interests in keeping the Game working (the settings file) and, if enabled, in crash diagnostics and anonymous gameplay analytics; and on your consent for personalised advertising, collected through Google’s consent form, which you may withdraw at any time.

Service providers & international transfers

We share limited data only with providers that process it on our behalf and only for the services that are active: GitHub (website hosting), Google Play and the Apple App Store, and — if enabled — Sentry, GameAnalytics and Google AdMob. Each processes data under its own privacy policy. For EEA/UK users, transfers outside the EEA/UK rely on adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your choices & rights

  • Uninstalling the Game deletes all progress stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to certain processing and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • To exercise a right, email hello@midnightsnack.games. Because the Game has no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find data linked to your installation.

Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

Data retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete the Game or clear its data. If the optional services are enabled, crash reports and analytics are kept only as long as needed to fix problems and improve the Game, then deleted or aggregated.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@midnightsnack.games.
