Launch News Sign-up

Privacy notice · Midnight Snack Games · Last updated: October 20, 2026

Short version: if you leave your email in a “Get launch news” form in one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, we use it only to tell you when our cozy cooking game comes out. We don’t sell it, we don’t share it for anyone else’s marketing, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who we are

Midnight Snack Games is a small independent studio. We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact: hello@midnightsnack.games.

Why you saw the ad

Our next game is a cozy cooking game that is still in development. Before release we show short clips of it with different dishes to learn which ones people like most. Whatever dish the clip showed, you will hear from us about the one game we actually release.

What we collect

  • The email address you submit in the form.
  • Which ad and form you used, and when — Meta passes this along with the email.

We don’t ask for your name, phone number, location or anything else.

How we use it

  • To send you the launch announcement, and at most a few short updates before it.
  • To count how many people signed up from each clip.

Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Legal basis (EEA/UK)

Your consent, given when you submit the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who processes it

The form runs on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) under Meta’s Privacy Policy. We download the submissions and keep them only for the purposes above. If we use an email service to send the announcement, it processes your address on our behalf only.

How long we keep it

Until the launch announcement is sent, and no longer than 12 months after you signed up. Then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your email, or to stop receiving messages, by writing to hello@midnightsnack.games. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The sign-up is for people aged 18 and over; our ads are shown only to adults.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
